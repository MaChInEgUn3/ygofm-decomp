External naming leads — UNVERIFIED
==================================

Same contract as the `UNVERIFIED datacrystal/` annotations in variables.h and
the `ygofm-community` RAG collection: everything here is a hypothesis to check
against a listing, never evidence, and it must never be ranked beside a
measured paragraph. Nothing below has been used to justify a line of C.

Source
------
https://github.com/Unchiga/YuGiOhForbiddenMemoriesRecomp — a static
recompilation of the same binary (SLUS-01411 = our SLUS_014.11), built on
PSXRecomp. Different technique entirely: the MIPS is translated to C ahead of
time and compiled native, with no byte-exactness requirement anywhere. It
therefore cannot help a single codegen decision. What it has is ~300 KB of
hand-written enhancement code that reads the game's live memory, commented.

Its LICENSE is NOASSERTION. **Do not copy its code or its prose.** Addresses
and strides of a third party's game are facts, not their authorship; their
sentences are theirs. Everything below is our own restatement, and every
structural claim was re-derived from our own listings before being written.

What was actually new, honestly
-------------------------------
Less than it first looked. The two structural claims worth checking both
turned out to be things this repo already had:

  * "the weight table is tiered, `0x8017878C + tier*1460`" — our own
    PARKED.txt entry for func_80021810 already said "walks 0x2D2 halfwords of
    a 1460-byte record". Re-derived independently anyway: the shift chain at
    the head of func_80021810 is 8a → 9a → 72a → 73a → 292a → 365a → 1460a,
    so the stride is 1460 exactly, and D_8017878C − D_801781D8 = 1460.
  * their "walks 722" is CORRECT and was mis-checked here at first: 0x2D2 is
    func_80021810's bound. func_800243F4's is 0x2D0. Two functions, two
    bounds. The error was ours, not theirs.

The one genuinely new thing is a cross-reference we had never written down:
**func_80021810 and func_800243F4 run the same weighted pick over the same
table family** — both `(func_8008E590() & 0x7FF) + 1` against a running total
of halfword weights — at different tiers. Recorded in variables.h beside
D_801781D8 and in both park entries.

How to use this
---------------
Only for comprehension, and only on the big remaining functions — knowing a
loop walks the fusion table tells you its bounds before you write a line. If a
name here ever turns out to be worth keeping, verify it against behaviour
first and then annotate the symbol in the usual place; do not promote anything
out of this file by citation alone.
