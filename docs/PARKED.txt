  inside the loop, and folding the offsets any other way moves the fault rather
  than removing it:
  - `base + i*2 + 0x18000 + 0x3BC4` (the literal reading) folds both constants
    into 0x1BBC4 and materialises it whole -- 55;
  - a `vram = D_8015C424 + 0x18000` local gets the immediates right (15300 and
    15216, as retail) but folds 0x18000 into the induction variable, where
    retail keeps the IV over `base` and adds $s5 in the loop -- 46, the best;
  - `q = base + i*2; q += 0x18000;` as separate statements -- 48;
  - writing 0x18000 before the index -- 55.
  Also needed and already applied: a `base` local for D_8015C424, or cc1psx
  folds the first reference's offset into %lo (the func_800300C8 lever).
  Flag sweep on the literal form: nothing below 53.

func_8002ABB4 -- 22 positional differences, all scheduling; the instruction
  count is right.
  Clones an object: allocates through func_800400AC(func_8004002C(), 6),
  copies +0x48/+0x18/+0x30/+0x20/+0x44 across, doubles the two halfwords at

  func_8002A9C0 on +0x4C and rewrites the flags at +4. The tail's `$v0` base

  agree and the whole diff is alignment:

func_8002D458 -- 42 differences, all of them the split-vs-bare barrier, and the
  first jump-table function to hit it.
  An eleven-case `switch` on arg0 setting D_8009B26C. The table, the dispatch,
  every case body and the two cross-jumped tails all match under plain
  -O2 -G8. What does not: retail stores D_8009B27A, D_8009B3D4, D_8009B0D1 and
  D_8009B365 through `lui $at` -- the assembler expanding a bare symbol -- while
  storing and loading D_801D07DC through an ordinary register with %lo folded,
  which is cc1psx's own pair. One file cannot produce both.
  The other escape does not work either, and this was measured rather than
  assumed: a -G8 compiler with a **-G0 assembler** (the recipe that matched
  func_800388D8 the same day) gives the four $at stores, but it also takes
  D_8009B268/D_8009B26C/D_8009B26D out of the gp-relative form retail has for
  them -- 79 differences. The function needs three addressing forms and there
  are only two knobs.
  **A jump-table function cannot take -mno-split-addresses at all.** Under it
  the table load itself becomes `lui $at,%hi(.rodata)` / `addu $at,$at,$v0` /
  `lw $v0,%lo(.rodata)($at)` where retail has the base in an ordinary register
  and `lw $v0,0($v1)`. So the usual escape for $at stores is closed for this
  whole class, and 17 of the 34 remaining jump-table functions contain no
  `lui $at` at all -- those are the ones to take first.
  Getting to 42: `case 1:` and `case 10:` have to be written out even though
  both do what `default` does, or gcc emits `sltiu $v0,$a0,10` where retail has
  11 -- the table covers 0..10 with two entries pointing at the default block,
  and the range check follows the *written* case range, not the reachable one.

func_8005C388 -- 11 differences, register assignment around the table base.

  every earlier pair, and hands the result to func_8005B8A0 through a
  func_8007E600/func_8007E710 pair. Instruction-for-instruction right; retail
  keeps the base in $a1 and the running index in $v1, we get the base in $v1
  and the loop pointer in $a0, and gcc hoists the loop pointer's initialisation
  above the counter's where retail has it after.
  44 -> 11, and each step is a lever already in WORKFLOW.md:
  - the base needs a local, or cc1psx folds the first reference's `+2` into
    `%lo(D_8009151C+2)` and every later offset is 2 too small (4 differences,
    but the wrong 4);
